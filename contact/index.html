Who sees my details?

Only the practice’s team, to reply to your enquiry. Your details are not shared or used for anything else.

Can I enquire from outside India?

Yes. Leave your number with the country code, or message on WhatsApp, and mention your time zone.

What are the consultation and program fees?

Please ask for the current fees when you enquire. The consultation and the six-month program are separate options, and the team explains the scope and the fee before you decide.

Will I be pushed to buy a remedy?

No. In her words: “It is not a business of fear.” Where a remedy can help she explains why, and the decision stays with you.
