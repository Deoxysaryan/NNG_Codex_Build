Before you enquire

A few things you may want to know first.

Ask something else
How often will we speak?

The rhythm of calls is agreed at the start, around your life. Messages fill the space in between.

Can my family be part of it?

Yes. Many questions involve a partner, children or parents, and the program can take them into account.

Can I start with a single consultation?

Yes. You can begin with one consultation and decide about the program afterwards.

Can I take part from outside India?

Yes. Calls and messages work across time zones. Mention yours when you enquire.

What does the program cost?

The fee depends on the format and is shared when you enquire, before you decide.

Does this replace medical or professional advice?

No. This is personal and spiritual guidance. It does not replace medical care, mental health treatment, legal advice or financial advice from qualified professionals.
