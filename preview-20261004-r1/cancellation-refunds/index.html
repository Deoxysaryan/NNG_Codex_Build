01

Current review status

This draft incorporates NNG's confirmed full-payment requirement and commercial no-refund policy for client cancellations. It also records the monthly contact format for the Hand Holding Program. Outstanding cases are explicitly marked below.

These pages do not collect payment. The final terms must be explained before a new booking is paid for. This review does not retrospectively replace an existing client agreement. Existing terms and applicable law remain relevant.

02

Consultations: changes, cancellations and no-shows

If a client cancels a consultation, NNG's commercial policy is that the fee is not refunded. If Narayani cannot attend the scheduled session, NNG will arrange a replacement appointment with the client.

Nothing in this policy excludes a refund or other remedy required by applicable law, including for non-delivery, deficient service or misrepresentation.

Still awaiting confirmation

Client-requested rescheduling: required notice and permitted changes. Late arrival and missed sessions. Technical or connection failures. The resolution where NNG cannot deliver the session or no suitable replacement appointment can be agreed.

For a change request, contact the team promptly with the booking reference, appointment date and time zone. This draft does not create an automatic no-show forfeiture or an unlimited rescheduling entitlement.

03

Hand Holding Program: contact, commitment and changes

The six-month program includes one scheduled conversation per month, by call or face to face as agreed, plus unlimited messages and recorded replies during the agreed tenure. Unlimited messaging does not promise instant responses, unlimited live sessions, 24/7 support or emergency care.

NNG expects clients to commit to the full agreed tenure. This reply does not settle all pause, early-exit or unused-session cases. The written program confirmation must explain those rules before enrolment.

Still awaiting confirmation

Communication channels, contact hours and expected response time; whether pauses are allowed; exact early-exit and unused-session treatment; exceptional circumstances; and what happens if NNG cannot continue.

Nothing in this policy excludes a refund or other remedy required by applicable law, including for non-delivery, deficient service or misrepresentation. An enquiry does not authorise automatic renewal or recurring charges.

04

Reports and personalised work

A report is offered only when its contents, preparation start, delivery channel and turnaround have been confirmed. Flag incorrect inputs promptly. If a file cannot be opened or was sent to the wrong confirmed address, contact the team for assistance.

Awaiting NNG decision

Which reports are sold; when personalised preparation starts; the correction/rework process; cancellation before or after preparation; and a remedy for missed delivery commitments.

The personalised nature of work does not remove rights for non-delivery, a materially misdescribed deliverable or deficient service under applicable law.

05

Products: returns not yet finalised

NNG is currently focusing on consultations. Product sale and return arrangements will be addressed separately before product transactions are activated. Consultation cancellation terms do not establish a no-return policy for products.

Deferred until the product offering is ready

Seller and invoicing responsibility, product disclosures, return address, change-of-mind rules, damaged or incorrect items, return shipping, replacements and refunds.

Any already accepted order remains subject to its agreed terms and applicable consumer rights.

06

Product delivery: arrangements deferred

Delivery regions, dispatch estimates, charges, courier partners, tracking, transit damage, loss and international shipping arrangements are not yet approved. No delivery commitment is created by these review pages. Product payments or orders should not be accepted until the relevant terms are confirmed and disclosed.

07

Requesting a resolution or refund

For a complaint or refund dispute, email enquire@nngarg.com. Include your name, booking/order reference, transaction date, issue and the resolution you are seeking. Share proof of payment with unnecessary financial details hidden. Never send an OTP, card security code or banking password.

For routine booking changes or order updates, use the general enquiry channel: enquiry@nngarg.com or +91 92055 11101. Team NNG currently handles grievances and complaints internally, by email only.

NNG accepts UPI, bank transfer and payment links and issues the bill or invoice. For a refund request, the team should confirm the request reference, its decision and reasons, any amount approved, the refund method and expected processing date. Approved refunds should ordinarily be returned through the original payment route, subject to lawful and operational requirements. The refund processing timetable remains to be confirmed.

No provision here restricts access to a competent consumer forum, regulator or other lawful remedy. Team NNG currently handles complaints through the email-only channel above. Formal response and escalation arrangements remain to be finalised.
