01

Current review status

The website currently invites consultation and product enquiries. It does not take payment through these pages. An enquiry or basket is not a confirmed appointment or order.

NNG has not confirmed the policy decisions shown below. This page is a review draft, not a new cancellation agreement for existing clients. Any existing written booking or order terms remain relevant, subject to applicable law. New customers should receive the approved terms before payment.

02

Consultations: changes, lateness and no-shows

If you need to move or cancel an appointment, contact the team as early as possible using the channel in your booking confirmation. Include the booking reference, appointment time and time zone, and whether you want a new date or cancellation.

Awaiting NNG decision

Notice period; number of permitted reschedules; late-arrival and no-show treatment; cancellation charges; treatment of an advance; and refunds where preparation has begun.

The policy must also cover cancellation by NNG and connection failures affecting an online session. No fee or forfeiture is created by this draft. Consumer remedies for an undelivered or deficient service remain available as applicable.

03

Hand Holding Program: pause or early exit

A six-month programme needs its own written schedule and payment terms. Contact the team if you need to pause or exit. Request a statement of sessions and work delivered, payments received, and any proposed treatment of the unused portion.

Awaiting NNG decision

Eligibility and duration of a pause; early-exit notice; unused-session treatment; instalment cancellation; medical or other exceptional circumstances; and what happens if NNG cannot continue the programme.

Any approved rule must be explained before enrolment. A programme enquiry does not authorise automatic renewal or recurring charges.

04

Reports and personalised work

A report is offered only when its contents, preparation start, delivery channel and turnaround have been confirmed. Flag incorrect inputs promptly. If a file cannot be opened or was sent to the wrong confirmed address, contact the team for assistance.

Awaiting NNG decision

Which reports are sold; when personalised preparation starts; the correction/rework process; cancellation before or after preparation; and a remedy for missed delivery commitments.

The personalised nature of work does not remove rights for non-delivery, a materially misdescribed deliverable or deficient service under applicable law.

05

Gemstones, Rudraksha and crystals: returns

Before paying, confirm the exact piece, material and treatment disclosures, size/weight, certificate if applicable, seller, return address and return conditions. Ask whether sizing, setting, engraving or another customisation changes change-of-mind eligibility.

If the item arrives damaged, wrong or materially different from the agreed description, contact the team promptly with the order reference and helpful photographs. Keep the packaging where practical so the issue can be checked. An unboxing video is not made a mandatory condition of a claim by this draft. Do not post a return until the return destination and process are confirmed.

Awaiting NNG decision

Change-of-mind return window; permitted handling; exclusions for customised items; return authorisation; inspection; return shipping cost; replacement choices; and refund processing time.

Natural variation must be disclosed and does not excuse an item that fails to match the agreed material, specification or description. Do not assume a spiritual product has no consumer protection.

06

Shipping and delivery

The team must confirm stock, dispatch estimate, delivery region, delivery charges, tracking, applicable taxes and expected arrival before accepting an order. International orders need an agreed destination, importer/customs responsibilities and any applicable restrictions.

Awaiting NNG decision

Dispatch time; delivery partners and regions; free-shipping rules if any; handling of loss, delay or transit damage; unsuccessful delivery; international duties; and cancellation before dispatch.

No delivery time or overseas availability is promised by this review page. Where an accepted order cannot be supplied, the team must address the issue under the agreed terms and applicable law.

07

Requesting a resolution or refund

Email enquiry@nngarg.com or contact the team on +91 92055 11101. Include your name, booking/order reference, transaction date, issue and the resolution you are seeking. Share proof of payment with unnecessary financial details hidden. Never send an OTP, card security code or banking password.

The team should confirm the request reference, its decision and reasons, any amount approved, the refund method and the expected processing date. Approved refunds should ordinarily be returned through the original payment route, subject to lawful and operational requirements. NNG must confirm its payment provider and processing timetable before this policy is activated.

No provision here restricts access to a competent consumer forum, regulator or other lawful remedy. The named grievance officer and formal response arrangements remain to be confirmed.
