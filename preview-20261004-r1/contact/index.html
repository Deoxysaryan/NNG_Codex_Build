Who sees my details?

WhatsApp and email handle the messages you send through them. This website does not save the details you enter in its WhatsApp enquiry flow. Please avoid sending medical records or other sensitive documents in an initial enquiry.

Can I enquire from outside India?

Yes. Message on WhatsApp or email and mention your country and time zone.

What do the consultations cost?

Online, 15 minutes: ₹11,000. Online, 45 minutes: ₹21,000. In person, 30 minutes: ₹25,000. In person, 1 hour 15 minutes: ₹50,000. Each fee is for one session, and the team confirms the format and final amount before you book. The fee for the Personalised Hand Holding Program is shared when you enquire.

Will I be pushed to buy a remedy?

No. In her words: “It is not a business of fear.” Where a remedy can help she explains why, and the decision stays with you.
