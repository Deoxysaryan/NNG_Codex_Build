01

Nature of the services

Brainology / Psychic Reader, Astrology, Numerology and Vastu / Vastulogy are presented here as personal or spiritual guidance and interpretive traditions. Brainology is NNG’s service terminology; it is not a claim that the service is neuroscience, clinical psychology or an evidence-based medical treatment.

The Personalised Hand Holding Program is ongoing guidance within the scope agreed with the team. It is not emergency support or a substitute for professional treatment. Discuss the approach, its limitations and what will actually be included before booking.

02

Health and Body Vastu

References to health, wellbeing, stress or Body Vastu do not offer diagnosis, cure, treatment or medical advice. Do not stop prescribed treatment, alter medication, delay a medical assessment or disregard qualified advice because of a consultation, remedy, video or article. Seek appropriate professional help for medical or mental health concerns.

03

Money, relationships, career and property

No reading guarantees income, investment performance, a job, business success, reconciliation, marriage, a legal result or a change in another person’s behaviour. Obtain qualified legal, financial or other professional advice for decisions in those areas.

Vastu guidance does not certify structural safety, property title, planning permission or investment suitability. A qualified professional must assess physical changes and regulatory requirements. Interpretations should not be used to make accusations about a partner, employee or family member.

04

Remedies and products

Gemstones, Rudraksha, crystals and related items may be chosen for personal or spiritual practice. Their purchase does not guarantee a health, relationship, career or financial effect. Ask why a remedy is suggested and consider your own budget. Buying a product is not a condition of browsing educational material or submitting a consultation enquiry.

A product’s authenticity, material or laboratory certificate is separate from any spiritual interpretation. Confirm the evidence and exact item before purchasing.

05

Testimonials and educational content

Testimonials describe individual experiences. They do not establish typical results, guaranteed outcomes or endorsement by a client’s employer. Articles, diagrams, demonstration reports and videos provide general information; a demo is not a personalised consultation.

Public content may simplify a subject and may be updated. Confirm the currently offered service, availability and written deliverables with NNG before relying on a description when booking. A title used for a practitioner does not expand the clinical or regulated scope described here.
