BEFORE BOOKING

A few practical questions.

What if I do not know my birth time?

Say so in your enquiry. A missing or approximate time affects what can be read. The team can explain the relevant options; do not invent an exact time.

Will the reading tell me what to do?

Narayani offers an interpretation and questions to consider alongside your real circumstances. A chart does not make a career, relationship, legal or financial decision for you.

What are the fee and appointment details?

The team confirms the reading’s scope, online or in-person format, duration, current fee and availability before you book. This page does not reserve a session or take payment.

Can I share someone else’s birth details?

Obtain that adult’s permission first. A parent or guardian must consent for a child. No third-party birth information is requested on this page.

Astrology is an interpretive tradition, not a reliable prediction or a substitute for professional advice. A reading cannot guarantee a promotion, marriage, business profit or legal outcome.
