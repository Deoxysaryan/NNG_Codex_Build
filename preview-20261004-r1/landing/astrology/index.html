BEFORE YOU DECIDE

The practical questions.

What if I do not know my birth time?

Tell the team that it is uncertain and how it was estimated. Ask which parts of a reading are possible; do not invent an exact time.

What should I prepare?

A short question and your own date, time and place of birth, when requested by the team. No birth data is collected on this page.

Can I share a partner’s or child’s details?

Obtain permission before sharing another adult’s information. A parent or guardian must consent for a child. The team should explain the required details and handling first.

What are the fee and appointment details?

Confirm scope, format, duration, current fees and availability before you book. This page does not take payment or reserve an appointment.
