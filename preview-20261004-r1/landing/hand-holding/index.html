BEFORE BOOKING

A few practical questions.

Can I begin with one consultation?

Yes. A focused consultation is a separate option. You do not have to commit to six months to begin working with Narayani.

How often will we speak?

The programme runs for six months. Confirm the contact rhythm, response arrangements, inclusions and boundaries with the team before committing.

Can we enquire as a couple or from outside India?

Yes. The main NNG programme offers individual and couple formats, and online guidance outside India. Mention your preferred format, country and time zone.

What is the fee and when can I start?

Ask the team about the next available start date and the fee for your format. Confirm payment, cancellation and refund terms before making a commitment.
