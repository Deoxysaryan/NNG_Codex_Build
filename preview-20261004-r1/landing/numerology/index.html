Must I change my name or number?

No. Begin by discussing the interpretation and your reason for considering a change. A change should not be treated as mandatory or a guarantee.

Yes. Proposed business names are a topic on NNG’s main site. Ask the team what information and scope are appropriate for your consultation before sharing the shortlist.

No. This landing page is for consultation enquiries. Confirm separately whether a suitable report is offered and what it contains before agreeing to any purchase.
