BEFORE BOOKING

A few practical questions.

Do I need a clear question?

One situation is enough to begin an enquiry. The team can help you identify which consultation is relevant; you do not need to select every practice.

Can I start without joining a programme?

Yes. A focused consultation and the six-month Hand Holding Program are separate choices. You can enquire about a consultation on its own.

What are the format, duration and fee?

Ask the team for the details of the consultation you choose. Online and in-person appointments are discussed with availability, scope, duration and current fees before booking.

No. This page does not collect your name, birth details, medical records or third-party information. Start with a general enquiry and share further details only when appropriate.
