BEFORE BOOKING

A few practical questions.

Do I need a floor plan to enquire?

No upload is needed for the first message. After discussing scope, the team may request a plan with north marked, a sketch or relevant photographs.

Is desk guidance the same as a full consultation?

No. Say whether you want desk-focused guidance or a wider workplace review. The team will explain the scope and fee for that enquiry.

Should I send a video of my home?

Not with your first enquiry. Agree any footage requirements, privacy arrangements and non-video alternative with the team before sharing private home material.

What are the format and fees?

Confirm the property type, scope, appointment format, any visit or report arrangements, duration and fee with the team. This page does not sell a property or desk report.
