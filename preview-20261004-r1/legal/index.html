Confirmed terms and remaining decisions

NNG has confirmed full payment before booking, a commercial no-refund policy for client cancellations subject to applicable law, rescheduling when Narayani cannot attend, and one monthly call or face-to-face conversation plus unlimited messages and recorded replies for the Hand Holding Program.

These points still need confirmation before the policies take effect:

  • Client changes and missed sessions: What happens if a client reschedules, is late, misses the session or loses internet access? How much notice is needed?
  • NNG cannot deliver: What resolution is offered if no suitable replacement appointment can be agreed or NNG cannot finish a program?
  • Hand Holding details: Which channels, days/hours and expected reply time apply? Can a program be paused? Confirm early-exit and unused-session rules.
  • Privacy arrangements: Where are records stored, who can access them, which tools generate reports, are sessions recorded, what client data reaches AI tools, and when is information deleted?
  • Final review: Confirm formal complaint response/escalation arrangements and obtain legal review before activation. Product rules are deferred until product sales are ready.

These are public review drafts, not a certification of legal compliance. The operating arrangements and final wording require approval before activation.
