Who is responsible

The website behaviour described below was checked on 15 October 2026. Grievances are handled by email only at enquire@nngarg.com. The responsible officer’s name and designation, retention schedule and approved service providers remain pending. The proposed handling of information after it reaches the team requires operational confirmation before this policy becomes effective.

02

When you browse or prepare an enquiry

This is a static review website hosted on GitHub Pages. Its hosting and network providers may process technical information such as your IP address, browser information and requested pages to deliver and secure the site, under their own policies.

The WhatsApp enquiry selector prepares text in your browser. It does not send that text to an NNG website database. WhatsApp links include the service or selected topic, country choice where selected, and enquiry source; a shop enquiry can include selected products and quantities. Opening WhatsApp hands the prepared text to WhatsApp. You decide whether to send it. WhatsApp and your email provider process information under their own terms.

No analytics or advertising tracker was observed in the pages and shared scripts inspected for this release. This is a dated check, not a promise about future releases. See “Browser storage & external media” for other technical processing.

03

When you contact or book with the team

Once you send a message or email, the team receives the contents you send, your contact details and any attachments or conversation history available through that channel. We may need your name, phone or email, country/time zone, selected service and appointment preferences to respond and arrange a booking.

For an agreed Astrology or Numerology service, relevant inputs may include names, birth date, birth time and birthplace. For Vastu, the agreed inputs may include a floor plan, directions, measurements and limited property photographs. A product order may require delivery and billing details, order records and payment confirmation. The exact inputs should be explained before collection; do not provide everything listed here by default.

Avoid sending medical reports, intimate details, government identity documents or banking credentials in an initial enquiry. If sensitive information is genuinely necessary for a lawful, agreed purpose, the team must first explain that purpose, intended recipients and handling arrangements and obtain the required consent.

04

Why information is used

The proposed purposes are to answer your enquiry, determine the appropriate service, prepare and deliver an agreed consultation or report, manage an agreed programme, process an accepted product order, keep required transaction records and resolve complaints.

Collecting information for a consultation does not automatically authorise promotional messages, advertising audience uploads, testimonials, model training or unrelated use. Marketing must be optional with a separate choice and a workable opt-out. This review does not introduce an AI companion or authorise use of private consultations to train AI.

05

Another person’s details and children

Do not share a partner’s, colleague’s, employee’s or other adult’s birth details or private records without their permission or other valid authority. Where their information is necessary, the team should confirm the permitted purpose and collect only the minimum required. Your enquiry is not a substitute for that person’s consent.

Bookings and purchases are handled by adults aged 18 or over. A request concerning a child must be managed by a parent or lawful guardian, with authority verified and any required consent obtained before collection. This site has no child account or direct child-booking flow. Do not send a child’s photograph, school report or medical record in an initial enquiry. Tell us if you believe a child’s information has been provided without authority.

06

Home footage, session recordings and testimonials

There is no home-video upload form on this review website. Do not send a home walkthrough unless the team has first agreed its necessity, recipient, access restrictions, retention period and deletion route with you. Ask whether a cropped floor plan, selected photographs or a discussion would be sufficient instead. Remove faces, documents, valuables, access codes and identifying information that is not needed.

Session recordings require a separate agreement about purpose, access and retention before recording begins. Consultation participation does not authorise publication. A testimonial, screenshot, photograph or client film should be used publicly only with permission covering that use. Request a review or withdrawal of permission through the contact below; previously distributed material and legally required records may require separate handling.

07

Who may receive information

For the proposed service process, access should be limited to Narayani and authorised NNG staff who need it for the agreed purpose. Necessary providers may include messaging/email services, appointment or record systems, payment providers and, for accepted shop orders, the disclosed supplier and delivery partner. NNG must confirm its actual provider list and access arrangements before activation.

Only the minimum required information should be shared. A courier does not need birth details or consultation notes. Suppliers do not need your private life history. Personal information is not offered for sale under this proposed policy. Lawful disclosures may be necessary to meet legal obligations, respond to competent authorities, prevent fraud or address a legal claim.

Some providers may process data outside India. The final policy must identify the relevant services and required safeguards. There is no claim here that all information stays in India or that every provider has already been independently audited.

08

Browser storage & external media

The shop uses local storage to keep selected product identifiers, variants and quantities on your device, and session storage for collection filters, search and page position. Some enquiry prompts use browser storage to remember that a prompt was shown or dismissed. These functions are different from advertising cookies. Clearing site storage removes saved choices; you can still contact the team by email.

Landing-page topic selections currently remain in page memory. Locally hosted videos are requested from the site host. External Google Drive or YouTube media may receive technical information when an external player is loaded. Some landing pages ask you to choose before loading an external player; other website media may have different behaviour. External links are governed by their providers’ policies.

If analytics, Meta Pixel, remarketing or other non-essential tracking is introduced, the actual tools and purposes must be disclosed and any required prior choice implemented before activation. This text alone does not provide consent. These new legal pages use no analytics, advertising scripts or external video players.

Provider notices: GitHub, WhatsApp and Google.

09

Retention, security and deletion

Information should be kept only for the agreed purpose and necessary legal, accounting or dispute requirements. NNG has not yet approved category-specific retention periods for enquiries, birth details, consultation notes, programme records or property media. Those periods, deletion owners and backup handling must be confirmed before routine collection. This draft does not promise automatic deletion after an invented number of days.

The proposed standard is restricted access, appropriately secured accounts and devices, careful provider selection and a documented incident process. No website or messaging service can guarantee absolute security. No ISO certification, end-to-end protection across every system or completed security audit is claimed here. Personal consultation information should never be placed in this public website repository.

10

Your choices and requests

You can ask about the information held, request correction or deletion, withdraw consent for an optional use, or opt out of marketing. Explain the request through enquiry@nngarg.com using “Privacy request” in the subject. Use the minimum details needed to locate your enquiry; do not attach identity documents unless a proportionate verification step is explained.

The team should explain any lawful reason for retaining information and any effect withdrawal has on a service that depends on it. Rights, complaint routes and response deadlines depend on the law applicable to the request. These draft choices are not a claim that every provision of every international privacy law applies to NNG. India’s data protection framework is being implemented in phases; the final notice and processes must reflect the provisions in force when activated.
