You may have a clear question about work, a relationship or your home. You may simply feel that the same difficulty keeps returning. You do not need to choose a practice before asking about the available options.

Narayani offers personal guidance through astrology, numerology and Vastu alongside mindset work. These pages explain the areas of guidance. The team can confirm what is available, what an appointment involves and any fee before you decide.

Questions to consider before you enquire

  • What keeps repeating, if anything
  • When the question started and what you have tried
  • What you would like help understanding

Start with what is happening

Write a sentence about the question you are carrying. If the same situation keeps returning, note two or three examples and what you would like to be different.

You can also note what you have already tried. These are prompts for your own preparation, rather than a required assessment or a promised session format.

Explore before you choose

The consultation page explains consultation options. The service pages offer background on the traditions and practices. Reading about a technique does not establish that Narayani offers it in a particular appointment.

Ask the team which option relates to your question. Confirm the fee, format and preparation before booking. You can take time to decide.

Keep the limits clear

Personal guidance does not promise a result or replace medical, mental health, legal or financial advice. Astrology, numerology and Vastu are interpretive traditions.

You do not need to include private records in an initial enquiry. Share only what helps you ask about an option.

A few notes for your first message

  • A sentence about your question
  • The option you are considering, if you have one
  • A question about fees, format or preparation

Confirm the option, format, fee and preparation with the team before booking.

Questions people ask

If yours is not here, ask it when you enquire.

Ask something else
Do I need to choose a practice first?

No. You can ask the team about relevant options before deciding. Say briefly what your question concerns.

Does this page offer a discovery session?

This page helps you prepare an enquiry. It does not confirm a separate discovery-session service. Ask the team what appointment options are currently available.

Should I send birth details straight away?

Ask what preparation your chosen consultation needs before sending personal details.

Is guidance online or in person?

Ask the team to confirm the available format for your chosen option.

Can someone join me?

Mention your preference when you enquire. The team can confirm what is possible for the appointment.

Will a result be guaranteed?

No. Personal guidance and interpretive traditions do not guarantee changes in health, relationships, career or finances.
