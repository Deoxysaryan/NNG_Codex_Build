Why the mind comes before the remedy

A remedy is an instruction: chant this, keep that in the north-east, wear this. An instruction has to be carried out on an ordinary day, by someone who may be tired, busy or doubtful. If that state of mind is never looked at, the instruction waits on a shelf. This is her reason for putting the mind first.

She does not tell anyone what will go wrong if they skip a step. She explains her reasoning and leaves the choice with you.

What a pattern is made of

A pattern is a small chain that repeats. Naming its parts makes it easier to see, and easier to interrupt at the one part you can reach.

The parts of the chain

A thought
The first words you tell yourself when the situation arrives, such as “not again” or “they will never listen”.
A belief
An assumption you take as true, such as “I am not someone who gets noticed”. Repeated thoughts can reinforce it, especially when you remember only the examples that seem to fit.
The words you use
“Always”, “never” and “should” narrow what seems possible. Changing a word does not change the situation, but it can change what you try next.
A reaction
What happens before you decide anything: the tight jaw, the quick answer, the silence. Reactions are fast because they have been rehearsed.
A habit
What you do next, again and again, because it is the quickest way out of the discomfort. Scrolling, snapping, saying yes, putting it off.
The loop
The result of the habit often seems to prove the first thought, and the chain starts again. This is why a pattern can feel like a fact about the world instead of a habit of the mind.

Seeing the chain is a way of finding the one link you can change.

A money example makes the distinction clear. You avoid opening a bill because you expect bad news. The delay adds uncertainty, and that uncertainty makes the next bill harder to open. The practical question is when and how to look at it with support. This is a reflection on avoidance, not advice about debt, investments or what you can afford; those questions belong with an appropriately qualified adviser.

Breathing, affirmations and habits have no page of their own. They are the small, ordinary part of the day, and the next section covers them.

Breathing, affirmations and habits in an ordinary day

A 2023 meta-analysis reported changes in self-reported stress after breathwork, but study quality and techniques varied. A later attention-controlled trial found no advantage for its slow-breathing routine. This page does not prescribe breath holding or a breathing pattern.

An affirmation can be a sentence to reflect on, rather than a promise. In a 2009 experiment, a positive statement left some participants with low self-esteem feeling worse. A sentence such as “I am learning to answer calmly” is an illustration, not a tested treatment.

Habit formation varies between people. A daily-habit study found a wide range of times to develop automaticity, rather than a universal deadline. An everyday example is putting a notebook beside your morning cup to remind you to note one task. No authored guide or tapping schedule is promised here.

Ask something else
Is Brain Training a substitute for medical or mental health care?

No. Brain Training works alongside any care you already have and never stands in for it. Narayani does not diagnose conditions, and please do not stop or change a medicine or any other treatment on the strength of anything on this site. If your health or your mood is a worry, see your doctor or a mental health specialist before anything else.

Is breathing practice safe?

This page does not assess individual safety or prescribe a breathing exercise. If you are considering a practice for a health concern, discuss it with a qualified professional.

Why might affirmations or manifestation practice seem not to work?

Repeating a sentence does not control events or replace action. A statement that feels untrue can add pressure rather than help. Start with something believable and one action you can take: a conversation, a boundary or a task you keep avoiding. Other people’s decisions and your circumstances still matter. If distress is persistent or affects daily life, seek qualified support rather than blaming your thoughts.

Do I need to believe in these practices before I try them?

No. A fair way to approach any of them is as an experiment: try it a few times, notice what changes, and drop it if nothing does. Some practices ask for more of you than others. The traditional Hawaiian process, for example, asks everyone taking part for sincere commitment, and some personal forms are prayers. You are never required to say words you do not mean.

More questions (5)
Do I need any background, or an earlier course?

No. A consultation starts from the question you bring, so you do not need a course, a book or any experience of these practices. If you have already tried NLP, tapping or Ho'oponopono somewhere else, mention it when you enquire and say what you found useful and what you did not.

Is there a group or a course?

The guidance on this page is personal, one conversation at a time, and no group or course is described here. If you would like continued guidance, the Personalised Hand Holding Program offers six months of personal guidance, and the team explains how it works before you commit.

How often should I practise between conversations?

There is no practice schedule promised here. Ask what a suggested practice involves before deciding. Habit research concerns variation between people, rather than a fixed number of days.

Can tapping and Ho'oponopono be practised together?

Both are available for personal guidance, but they are different practices. Discuss your concern with Narayani before deciding whether one or both is appropriate. Their availability does not mean they need to be combined or that either is a treatment.

Do I get a certificate, or can I learn to practise this on others?

These pages describe personal guidance for your own questions. They do not describe a practitioner course or offer a certificate. Certificates in this field are voluntary, and a certificate shows only that someone trained with the body that issued it. If you hope to train to work with other people, say so when you enquire.
