Start from what you are facing

You do not need to know the name of the practice you need. Find the line that sounds like your situation.

Work and money

  • I am not being recognised at work, or things feel stuck
  • I am weighing a job change, a promotion or a new direction
  • I want a clear plan for the next few years
  • I run a business and want guidance on growth and timing
  • I want to grow as a leader over the next few years, not just change jobs
  • I want my business's numbers and chart read together
  • Money comes in and goes out again, and I want to see the pattern

Study and children

  • I am choosing a stream, a course or a career path
  • Exams, focus and concentration are a worry

Marriage and relationships

  • Marriage is delayed, or I am looking for the right partner
  • We want to know how well our charts fit
  • The same pattern keeps repeating in my relationships
  • I want a conversation about a relationship question, without a chart as the starting point

Mind and wellbeing

  • I want to understand my stress patterns and rhythms
  • I want to change a habit or calm a racing mind
  • I want to try a practice such as NLP, tapping or Ho’oponopono

  • I want a guided session to settle myself and the space around me

Before you enquire

A few things you may want to know first.

Ask something else
Can one consultation cover more than one area?

Yes. Your question decides the approach, and one conversation can draw on the mind, your numbers, your chart and your surroundings together.

What will I be asked to share?

Only what your question needs: perhaps your full name and date of birth, or the layout of your home. The team tells you before the session.

Does she suggest remedies?

Where a remedy can help, she suggests one that fits your life and explains why. The mind comes first, because a remedy works when the mind does.

Are consultations available outside India?

Yes, online. Mention your country and time zone when you enquire.

Does Narayani teach courses?

Not on this website at present. Her guidance is personal: consultations, and the six-month Personalised Hand Holding Program. If courses are planned, the team will tell you when you enquire.

What do the consultations cost?

Online, 15 minutes: ₹11,000. Online, 45 minutes: ₹21,000. In person, 30 minutes: ₹25,000. In person, 1 hour 15 minutes: ₹50,000. Each fee is for one session, and the team confirms the format and final amount before you book. The fee for the Personalised Hand Holding Program is shared when you enquire.

Does this replace medical or professional advice?

No. This is personal and spiritual guidance. It does not replace medical care, mental health treatment, legal advice or financial advice from qualified professionals.
