01

Who these terms cover

Transformation with NNG is the trade name of SIMMI NITESH GARG, a proprietorship. Our business details appear on the Legal & contact page. “NNG”, “we” and “our” refer to this business. Narayani Garg is the practitioner presented on this website.

These draft terms cover the website, related landing pages, enquiries and the services or products specifically confirmed by NNG. This public staging version is for review. It does not replace an existing booking agreement or authorise payment. The final terms must be provided before a paid booking or order is accepted.

02

What you can consult about

Our offer includes Brainology / Psychic Reader consultations, Astrology, Numerology, Vastu / Vastulogy and the Personalised Hand Holding Program. Guidance may address questions about wellbeing, relationships, money, career, business or the use of a space. The Service Disclaimer explains the boundaries.

A service page introduces an area of guidance; it does not promise every method, report or deliverable. Before booking, confirm who conducts the session, its scope, language, format, duration, fee and any follow-up. A written report is included only when the booking confirmation says so.

03

Enquiries, booking and payment

WhatsApp and email links let you contact the team. Opening a prepared message, selecting a topic or adding an item to the basket does not reserve stock, book an appointment or take payment. Review the message before sending it.

A booking is confirmed only when the team has confirmed the appointment and applicable payment requirements in writing. Confirm the date, time zone, online or in-person format, total price including applicable taxes, payment recipient and receipt arrangements before paying. Use only payment instructions confirmed through the official NNG contact channels. Never share a banking password, card security code or one-time password with the team.

Final consultation fees, payment provider and service-specific cancellation terms are awaiting NNG confirmation. No automatic renewal or recurring debit is authorised by submitting an enquiry.

04

Information you provide

Provide accurate contact details and, where relevant, birth date, birth time, birthplace, names, property information or the question you want to discuss. Tell the team when a birth time or measurement is an estimate. Incorrect inputs can affect an interpretation; ask about corrections before work begins.

Share another adult’s information only with their permission or other valid authority. A parent or lawful guardian must handle a request involving a child. Do not send medical records, identity documents or private home footage in an initial enquiry. The Privacy Policy explains the proposed handling of personal information.

05

Personalised Hand Holding Program

The website describes ongoing personal guidance over six months. Admission, start and end dates, session frequency, communication channels, response hours, included practices or reports, fees and payment schedule must be set out in a separate written programme confirmation.

Enrolment does not imply unlimited access, round-the-clock support, emergency care, automatic renewal or a particular outcome. Programme pause, early-exit and unused-session rules are awaiting confirmation. Do not pay until the applicable terms have been explained and accepted.

06

Vastu, property inputs and remedies

Confirm whether the assignment covers a home, business premises, property selection, a desk or Body Vastu, and whether a visit or report is included. Body Vastu is a spiritual consultation; it is not a medical examination, diagnosis or treatment.

Vastu guidance is not a building survey, structural assessment, title check or planning approval. Consult qualified professionals before structural, electrical, legal or investment decisions. Purchasing a gemstone, Rudraksha, crystal or other remedy is a separate, optional decision. Ask for the reasoning, price and product terms before purchasing.

07

Gemstones, Rudraksha and crystals

The shop currently supports catalogue browsing and order enquiries, rather than payment checkout. The basket total is indicative. Availability, the exact piece, size, weight, material, treatments where relevant, certification, seller, total price, taxes, delivery and return terms must be confirmed before an order is accepted.

Catalogue photographs may show a reference piece. Natural products can vary, but the item supplied must match its agreed description. Certification, origin, laboratory testing or authenticity is promised only when supported by the specific order details. The Cancellation, Returns & Delivery page explains the review-stage position.

08

Changes, cancellations and complaints

Ask the team promptly if you need to change or cancel a session or order. Do not assume a notice period, fee, refund percentage or non-refundable deposit: those commercial rules are awaiting approval and must be disclosed before payment.

No term here removes a remedy available under applicable law for a defective or misdescribed product, deficient service, non-delivery or other actionable issue. Contact the team with the booking or order reference and your requested resolution.

09

Content, recordings and respectful use

Website text, artwork, videos and materials are protected by the rights of their respective owners. You may read the resources and use consultation materials for your own agreed personal purpose. Obtain permission before commercial redistribution, resale, impersonation or publication of a private session or another client’s information.

Any session recording requires an explicit agreement before recording begins. An enquiry or booking does not grant NNG permission to use your name, image, voice or story in advertising. Testimonial or marketing use requires separate permission. Do not harass the team, misuse the website, introduce malicious code or attempt unauthorised access.

No blanket liability waiver, compulsory arbitration or exclusive court clause is imposed in this review draft. Indian law applies subject to any mandatory protections and lawful remedies available to you.
