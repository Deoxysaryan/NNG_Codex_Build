# NNG Hinglish landing page review

Five service-wide landing pages: Brainology, Astrology, Numerology, Vastulogy and Hand Holding. The psychic-reader route is an alias to Brainology.

Build with `node build.mjs` (Node 18+; no package install required). Serve `dist/` with a static server. Content is in `content.mjs`, layout in `build.mjs`, styling in `styles.css`, and enquiry/privacy/motion behaviour in `app.js`. All assets needed to rebuild are included locally in `assets/`.

Deploy only the contents of `dist/` to `preview-20261013-copy-review/landing/` on the NNG_Codex_Build gh-pages branch. Do not overwrite the existing preview-20261004-r1 or production routes. Staging uses noindex,nofollow. No ad pixels, analytics, payments or lead storage are enabled.

WhatsApp buttons open a user-editable message to NNG's existing public number. They do not send automatically. The enquiry selector is not a submitted lead form. Fees, formats, scope and appointments remain enquiries until confirmed by NNG.

Proof: the 10,000+ client and global business-audience statements are user-confirmed. Testimonial summaries are labelled as summaries of broader Narayani guidance and linked to existing original videos; they are not service-specific outcome guarantees. Body Vastu scope, report contents and programme contact schedules remain unconfirmed and are not invented.

Competitor landing-page references: https://astroarunpandit.org/consultation/ and Aditya Kundali's consultation booking flow reached from https://adityakundali.com/. Adopted clear service categories, consultation explanation, source-linked proof and booking steps. Competitor success claims, prices and rituals were not copied. Static-ad references informed statics separately, not the landing-page service scope.
