Before you enquire

A few things you may want to know first.

Ask something else
Where should I begin?

With the question on your mind, not with a service. Message the team on WhatsApp, call or email. They explain the options, fees and next steps before you book.

How is a consultation different from the program?

A consultation focuses on a question or concern. The Personalised Hand Holding Program offers continued guidance over six months. Ask the team about its scope and how contact works.

Can I enquire if I live outside India?

Yes. Mention your country and time zone when you enquire so the team can arrange an online consultation that suits you.

What are the consultation and program fees?

Please ask for the current fees when you enquire. The consultation and the program lasting six months are separate options. The team explains the scope and fee before you decide.

Does this replace medical or professional advice?

No. This is personal and spiritual guidance. It does not replace medical care, mental health treatment, legal advice or financial advice from qualified professionals.
