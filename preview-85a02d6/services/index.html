Before you enquire

A few things you may want to know first.

Ask something else
Can one consultation cover more than one area?

Yes. Your question decides the approach, and one conversation can draw on the mind, your numbers, your chart and your surroundings together.

What will I be asked to share?

Only what your question needs: perhaps your full name and date of birth, or the layout of your home. The team tells you before the session.

Does she suggest remedies?

Where a remedy can help, she suggests one that fits your life and explains why. The mind comes first, because a remedy works when the mind does.

Are consultations available outside India?

Yes, online. Mention your country and time zone when you enquire.

What are the consultation and program fees?

Please ask for the current fees when you enquire. The consultation and the program lasting six months are separate options. The team explains the scope and fee before you decide.

Does this replace medical or professional advice?

No. This is personal and spiritual guidance. It does not replace medical care, mental health treatment, legal advice or financial advice from qualified professionals.
